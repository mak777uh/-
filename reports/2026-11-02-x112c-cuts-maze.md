## What the evening's cut work leaves

that, while changing the definition kind moves the level by 0.2–0.37 and the gap by 0.21–0.38. The line's central claim therefore holds at
cut 0.15, 0.20 and 0.25 in both cells, which is a stronger statement than the single-cut originals.
