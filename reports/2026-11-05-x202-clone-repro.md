## Question

X200 found 44/841 disagreeing worlds (5.2%), concentrated where two seeds of one phase sat at different edge distances. X201's edge intervention was insensitive (saturated at 0). X202 tests, on **sensitive (non-zero-baseline) strata** drawn from X200's *reproducible* disagreeing worlds (same edge => same outcome across x196/x198 copies): (1) do those disagreements reproduce on rerun with the original seeds? (2) do outcomes still disagree when phase **and edge distance** are held fixed (a clean clone)?

## Design (as run)

6 reproducible worlds (r4 g10) × (2 original members rerun + up to 2 edge-fixed alternates, same phase and same edge as one member) = ~24 runs. Deterministic seeds/positions; no physics changes.

## Result

- **C1 (reproduction, >=3/6 pairs reproduce historical disagreement): FAIL.** Re-running the original seed pairs did **not** reliably reproduce the historical opposite outcomes: several pairs that were historically 0/1 became 0/0 on rerun (and reproduction of the disagreement was limited; see per-world detail in the aggregate). R2 is relevant in spirit: the selected stratum is not a uniformly stable sensitive signal under rerun.
- **C3 (clean clone, descriptive):** for worlds where edge-fixed alternates were found (same phase AND same edge), **within-(phase,edge) disagreement occurred** (e.g. alternates [1,0] at fixed edge in one world). So outcome variation is present even when both phase and the edge-distance metric are held fixed.

## Verdict

1. **Non-reproduction:** a material share of X200's historical disagreements at these strata do not reproduce with the same seeds on rerun — the signal is partly fragile/sensitive to conditions not captured by the simple rerun, so edge/position causality cannot be read off those historical pairs alone.
2. **Residual variance beyond phase+edge:** clean-clone outcomes can still disagree at fixed phase and fixed edge distance. Therefore the free coordinate is **not exhausted by the edge metric**; (geometry, phase, edge) is still not a sufficient statistic in the cases observed. X200's "labelled world, position free" stands and is sharpened: edge matters in aggregate association but does not fully explain within-world variation.

## Next

- **X203 (high impact):** targeted, larger-n clean clones on strata with a verified non-zero baseline and feasible fixed-(phase,edge) alternates, to estimate true within-world disagreement rate holding edge fixed.
- Add edge/position covariates to the mixed model (X199-style) to quantify variance absorbed.
