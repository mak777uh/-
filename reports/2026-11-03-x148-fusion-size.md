# X148 — the size of the fusion product does not decide whether a seed divides

**Plan:** [`2026-11-03-x148-fusion-size-plan.md`](2026-11-03-x148-fusion-size-plan.md) (17:50). **Runs:** none — the
analysis reads the 75 full logs of X142 and its reused arms. **Aggregate:** `l1_field_x142_coalescence.json`, block
`fusion`.

## The question

X142 put the boundary between mutual coalescence (M) and absorption/persistence (A, P) at a surface gap of 8–9 cells,
and X145 refuted the simple clock story (a lone disc divides at 180 steps while a pair at gaps 1–8 is one body by step
50). The natural alternative was size: the fusion product divides when it is **large enough**, and the gap decides
whether it gets there.

## The answer: no

The comparison is within arms, between the seeds that divided at or before the moment the two seeded uids disappeared
and those that did not, using the summed area of the field one logged step before that moment (defined for every run,
unlike the area of the fusion product itself, which needs a single component).

| arm | seeds that divided | seeds that did not | median area, divided | median area, not divided | sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| radius 3, gap 9 | 2 | 3 | 267 | 268 | interleaved |
| radius 3, gap 10 | 3 | 2 | 297 | 287 | interleaved |
| radius 3, gap 11 | 3 | 2 | **242** | **290** | divided is **smaller** |

Three arms contain both outcomes — the only arms where the question can be asked within an arm — and in none of them
are the dividing seeds the larger ones. At gap 11 the ordering is the **opposite** of the size hypothesis. The
registered outcome that fires is *size does not rule*: 0 of 3 arms have the dividing seeds larger.

The medians across arms point the same way as the interleaved within-arm comparison: the arms where everything
coalesces (gaps 1–8) end with bodies of 99–267 cells, and the arms where seeds divide are not systematically above
them. Whatever decides division, it is not the area of the body at the event.

## Limits, stated before the numbers were read

- Three arms is what the sweep has; the sign test could only have reached p = 1/16 with a consistent sign in all four
  arms where both outcomes occur, and there are three. The result is therefore **"no support for size"**, not "size
  excluded at any particular power".
- The area is read one logging step before the event, so it is the body's size in a 10-step window, not at the instant
  of contact.
- Runs whose seeds do not disappear inside the horizon (pure persistence) have no event and are excluded; they are the
  arms at gaps 10–11 where both lineages live, which is a selection that could in principle hide a size effect at the
  top of the range.
- Nothing here says what **does** decide division. Shape, the timing of the contact, or the local `u`/`v` composition of
  the product are all still open; the next step is to look at the field around the product at the event, which needs
  saved frames rather than logs.

## Controls

The classification reproduces X142 exactly (M/A/P per arm identical, checked by re-running the same script — the fusion
block was added to it, not to a copy); every fusion area reported separately carries one component; the counts of
dividing seeds per arm (2, 3, 3) are the ones X142 recorded; and every number above is a median with its n printed.
