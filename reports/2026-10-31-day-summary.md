## 3. Five design flaws, all caught before a claim

| # | flaw |
|---|---|
| 1 | the stripe sat on the seed, so the world died |
| 2 | one snapshot per 250 steps — information cannot be estimated |
| 3 | the cue acted immediately: a cause, not a signal |
| 4 | the lead changed only a recorded label — `lead0/20/40` had identical dynamics |
| 5 | the omission test had no measurable behaviour and its positive control never fired |

And one valid experiment (`X48`): memory is connected, structures survive, and **conditional transfer entropy does not
exceed its own surrogate** at either relaxation time. Negative, and for the first time not confounded.

## 4. Two arithmetic facts that saved work

- **`b_crit = 0.0075`** — the break-even coefficient, and the working threshold is above it (0.010 still dies).
- **CV of growth = 0.333** — above our own 0.3 threshold, so `X53` must be **paired**; an unpaired readout would need
  174 seeds for a 10 % effect. `X53` was therefore preregistered, not run.
