# X92 — does size pay? Area against lifetime and against the number of daughters, from stored artifacts

**Why.** X91 closed the last way the inheritance result could have been an artefact of place: at both habitable shoulders a daughter follows
her mother rather than her neighbour. What is still missing is the leg that turns *resemblance* into *inheritance in the biological
sense*: whether the resemblance **pays** — whether a structure's size buys it persistence or descendants. X29 tried to measure that and
its meter was invalid (selection differential +0.148 in an arm where the trait cannot act), so the field line has no measurement of
consequence.

The stored artifacts already contain the ingredients: every uid's lifetime (`first`–`last`), its peak area, and the parent→child pairs from
which its number of daughters can be counted. This is an **analysis of stored artifacts** — X87's 60 worlds and X88's 20 at 0.040 — with
no new runs.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

Four cells (`F = 0.025`, `0.030`, `0.035` from X87, `0.040` from X88), twenty worlds each, split identity convention. Connectedness: each
cell must hold at least **500 uids** with a recorded lifetime and at least **300 parent→child pairs**; otherwise the cell cannot answer.

## 2. Positive control fires (numeric)

A relation between size and lifetime could be an artefact of the measurement itself — a bigger structure occupies more cells and may take
longer to disappear. **The control fires if** the null band is narrower than the effect: within-world shuffles of area (1 000 draws) give a
95th percentile of |ρ| **below 0.10** in every cell, so an observed coefficient of 0.15 is outside the null and the two are separable. If
the null band is wider than that, the cell is too small and the series is void.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

Per cell, over all uids: Spearman(peak area, lifetime) and Spearman(peak area, number of daughters). The second is also computed as a
**partial** correlation controlling lifetime, because a longer-lived uid has more chances to divide and the raw coefficient would mostly say
"older means more daughters".

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

With 500–3 000 uids per cell the standard error of a Spearman coefficient is 0.02–0.045, so a coefficient of **0.10** is 2–5σ and is the
declared resolution; the shuffle null is computed per cell rather than assumed. Cost: **no runs**, ~15 minutes of CPU.

## 5. Refusal rule (registered before the run)

**Void** if a cell has fewer than 500 uids or 300 pairs, or if the §2 null band exceeds 0.10.

**Size pays** if both coefficients are at least **0.10** and the daughters coefficient stays at least **0.10** after controlling lifetime,
in at least three cells.
**Size is decorative** if |ρ| < 0.05 in at least three cells for both readouts.
**Mixed** otherwise, printed cell by cell with the null band.
