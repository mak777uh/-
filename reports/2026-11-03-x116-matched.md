## The test

X115 found the larger sister's line alive at the end +0.250 / +0.137 more often. A larger structure has more cells and often **more daughters**, so it can
lose members and still be present — the advantage could be arithmetic. X116 matches sister pairs on the **number of daughters** and compares survival only
among matched pairs.

Both controls fired: the full-sample contrast reproduces X115 within 5 %, and matching removes the confound by construction (mean difference in daughters

## What the numbers say, precisely

**The advantage more than halves when the branch count is equalised**: 0.250 → 0.125 in the lattice and 0.137 → 0.044 in the maze. So **branch count
explains about half of the lattice advantage and about two-thirds of the maze advantage** — the arithmetic objection is partly right, and X115's headline
was partly a count of daughters.

**But it does not explain all of it.** The residual is positive with an interval excluding zero in both cells (104:25 and 39:11 among matched pairs). In the
lattice the residual (0.125) is above the declared resolution (0.10); **in the maze it is not** (0.044 against a 0.10 bar), so the honest phrasing there is
"a residual of unresolved size".

Registered verdict: **the advantage is not branch count** — the rule asked whether the matched interval excludes zero, and it does in both cells. The
report adds what the rule did not: the residual's *size* is above resolution in one cell and below it in the other.

## Where this leaves the day's claim

X115's claim should now be quoted with X116 beside it:

> the larger sister's line is **alive at the end more often** (+0.250 / +0.137), and **half to two-thirds of that is having more daughters**; after
> equalising the number of daughters the residual is +0.125 (above resolution) in the lattice and +0.044 (below resolution) in the maze.

So "size pays in survival" survives, but it is a smaller and more specific statement than the raw contrast suggested: most of the payment is the
arithmetic of more branches, and the part that is not arithmetic is strongest where the medium is a lattice.

## Caveats

- Matching discards about 45 % of the pairs (1 142 → 633, 1 195 → 631), so the matched subsets are not the full designs.
- Matching on the number of daughters does not match on **cells**, and the larger sister still has more cells; a second matching on area (within 10 %) is the
  natural next test.
- Survival is presence in one snapshot at the horizon; the maze barely survives at all (963 of 1 195 pairs have neither branch alive).
- One seed set, one box, one grid, the 0.20 cut.

## Next

- **Match on area as well as on daughters** — the remaining confound. If the residual then vanishes, the survival advantage is fully arithmetic; if it holds,
  the line has a genuine lineage effect in survival.
