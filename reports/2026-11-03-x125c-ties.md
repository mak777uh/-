in the other, and the line had been quoting the two as if they were one number. One rule differed: ties.

## What the generation-time result actually says

Three statements, all true on the same 1 142 / 1 195 pairs, and they are not rivals:

1. **In a majority of non-tied pairs the larger sister's generation time is the longer one** — 52.6 % in the lattice (outside a coin, CI 0.493…0.557 is barely
   so), 38.8 % in the maze (i.e. a minority: there the larger sister divides sooner).
2. **In magnitude the larger sister's generation time is shorter** — mean 1 037.7 against 1 450.1 steps in the lattice, a difference of −412 steps over all pairs
   and −530 over non-tied ones.
3. **The reason 1 and 2 coexist is the tail**: the small sister who does not divide lingers for thousands of steps. The larger sister's advantage is frequent and
   small when it appears; the smaller sister's advantage is rare and enormous.

So the sentence the line may publish is: *the larger sister usually divides a little sooner or a little later, but she never lingers — and her line reaches more
generations per step regardless (6.06 / 18.96 with the filter printed)*. What the line may not publish is "the larger sister lives longer" (the majority statement)
or "the larger sister's generation time is shorter" (the magnitude statement) without saying which one it is quoting.

## The three definitions, final

| quantity | definition | filter (always printed) | published statistic |
|---|---|---|---|
| generation time | uid's last logged step − first + 1, logged steps | none; ties reported | **fraction of non-tied pairs**, with the mean and its tail decomposition |
| depth | longest descendant chain below the uid, generations | none | **mean** paired difference |
| rate | `depth / generation time × 1000`, generations per 1 000 steps | both sisters' generation time ≥ 50 steps | **pair-weighted mean** difference, with its denominator (957 of 1 142; 638 of 1 195) |

The depth leg is the one number that has survived every check in this chain unchanged: +1.479 and +2.213 to three decimals, against X113's own values. It is the
consequence claim that can travel now.
