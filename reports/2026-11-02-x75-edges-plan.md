# X75 — is the shallow memory a property of *mixedness*? Depth curves at the band edges

**Question.** X74 found that the size memory decays fastest at `F = 0.035` (half-life 0.73 divisions against 1.47 at 0.030 and
1.61 at 0.040) and that the three obvious covariates — splits per structure, splits per area, size dispersion — are all monotone
in `F` and therefore cannot produce a U. What the boundary cell has and the others do not is **two coexisting length scales**
(X71–X71c). This series tests that reading directly: run the depth measurement at the **band edges**, where mixing is partial
rather than absent.

X71c measured mixing at the edges at a 0.2 second-mode cut: `F = 0.033` gives 3 of 5 worlds mixed, `F = 0.037` gives 4 of 5. So
the edges are "half-mixed" cells. If the shallow memory is a property of mixedness, their half-lives should be short (near the
boundary's 0.73); if it is a property of `F` itself (something else about 0.035), they should look like their outer neighbours
(1.4–1.6).

## 1. Manipulation is connected

The inflow moves the pattern and the mixing: X71c measured 3/5 and 4/5 mixed worlds at these two cells at the 0.2 cut. The
connectedness check is that the new cells reproduce the X74 protocol's genealogy yield — at least 200 lag-2 pairs per cell —
since a cell with no genealogies cannot carry a depth measurement (the X71 artifacts, for instance, have none).

## 2. Positive control fires

Two numbers, before the half-life is compared.

1. **Lag-1 in each cell ∈ [0.40, 0.60]** — the range measured in all three X74 cells (0.475–0.507). A cell outside it is
   measuring something else.
2. **Cross-lineage shuffle p95 ≤ 0.10** at every lag, recomputed per cell.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

Identical to X73/X74: Spearman(ancestor peak area, descendant peak area) at lags 1–3, `λ` as the geometric mean of `ρ₂/ρ₁` and
`ρ₃/ρ₂`, half-life `ln 0.5 / ln λ`. Two new cells at `F = 0.033` and `0.037`, **50-step logging** (required — the X71c runs at
these inflows logged every 1000 steps and carry zero division pairs), five worlds each.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

Ten runs, 6000 steps, 50-step logging, no field snapshots: measured 70–111 s per run in X74, so ~15 minutes. The X74 cells gave
~1 000 lag-2 pairs each; the Fisher band on `ρ` is ±0.05 and on `λ` about ±0.1, so half-lives differing by less than a factor of
1.3 are not distinguishable — the same limit as X74, stated in advance.

## 5. Refusal rule

**Void** for a cell if it has fewer than 200 lag-2 pairs, or lag-1 outside [0.40, 0.60], or shuffle p95 above 0.10.

The series **supports "shallow memory is a property of mixedness"** if **both** edges give a half-life **below 1.0** division.
It **refutes** that reading if **both** edges give a half-life **above 1.3**. Anything else — one edge shallow, one not — is
**inconclusive** and is reported with the two numbers, because that outcome would mean the boundary cell is special for a reason
neither mixing nor `F` alone captures.
