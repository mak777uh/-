# X136 — the wall to the cell: separations 14, 16, 17, reusing 12, 15, 18

**Why.** X134b placed the wall between **15 and 18 cells** with a seed-dependent placement and showed the seeds now differ where they should. Its own caveat says the wall could be at 16 or 17. This run closes that: three new separations fill the gap at one-cell spacing, and the three X134b arms are **reused** rather than repeated, because the probe, the flags, the inflow and the seeds are identical.

The question is not "is there a wall" — X134b settled that — but **where** it is, and whether it is a wall at all or a gradient with a 50 % crossing somewhere inside.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

Same probe (`--init-spots R:SEP`, seed-dependent placement, anchored), same inflow `F = 0.030`, same radius (3 cells, 29 cells of area), same seeds 601–605, same 6 000 steps and dense logging.

| separation | runs | source |
|---|---|---|
| 12, 15, 18 | 15 | **reused from X134b** (`x134b_sep*_s*.json`, same probe and flags) |
| **14, 16, 17** | **15 new** | this run |

Thirty runs in the analysis, five of them new per separation. The readout is X134b's: one seeded subtree disappearing into the other's reach, and both subtrees present at the last step.

## 2. Positive control fires (numeric)

Five controls. **The control fires if** all five hold:

1. **The anchor still matches** (the probe has not changed since X134b): the X134b anchor against the committed projection of `x108_f030_s301.json`, key by key.
2. **The reused arms reproduce X134b exactly**: 12 → 5/5 absorbed, 15 → 5/5, 18 → 0/5, with the same per-seed merge steps (20,20,20,20,20 and 60,50,60,50,50). A change here means the analysis, not the world, has moved.
3. **The new arms start as two components** of area ≥ 20 cells at the first logged step.
4. **Every run is alive** under the structural rule.
5. **The order is monotone or reported as such**: merging at a larger separation while not merging at a smaller one is a non-monotonicity and is printed, not smoothed.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

Per separation: absorbed count out of five, both-alive count, median steps, per-seed steps. The five separations 14–18 bracket the crossing; with 5 seeds the resolution is three in five.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

One-cell spacing, five seeds per point. The declared resolution is **one cell** for the bracket's edges and **two seeds in five** for a point's classification. If the crossing lands between 15 and 16, the bracket becomes 15–16; if 14 also merges and 16 does not, it becomes 15–16 as well with 14 inside the wavelength. If **both** 16 and 17 merge (i.e. the wall is beyond 17), that contradicts X134b's 18-arm and the run is void by control 2 rather than a new result.

## 5. Refusal rule (fixed before the run)

**Void** if the anchor moves, or the reused arms miss their X134b values, or any new arm does not start as two components, or more than one run is dead.

**The wall is at cell N** if every separation below N merges in ≥ 3 of 5 seeds and every separation from N up does not, for the five separations measured. **A gradient** is reported if the per-seed merge steps rise smoothly across 14–18 without a clean 3/5 boundary; in that case the mixing length is quoted as the median crossing between the last merging and the first non-merging separation, and the word "wall" is dropped.
