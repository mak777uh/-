The association is positive — the direction the law predicts — in **9 of 11** cells
where the local variable has spread. The plan's refusal rule was written with that sign the wrong way round, and the mistake is
recorded in the aggregate rather than reinterpreted quietly.

**The cliff is not a site draw, but its arms are not the same size.** The gap-9 arm reads 0.37 over
54 runs and the gap-10 arm 0.00 over 24, and their mean local inflows
differ by **0.00272**, inside the registered bar of 0.004 — both sat in the same medium on average. But
their measured radii are **5.863** and **5.997**: the rasterisation of the
seeded disc depends on the parity of the separation, and the **larger** body is the one that did *not* fuse, so the cliff is not
an artefact of that difference — the difference makes the observed fall conservative.

**Limits.** The pooled deviance win can be a restating of X183 across cells that differ in rate by design, which is why the
per-cell column is reported next to it; a per-cell slope over 15-79 runs is a description, not a test. The cliff audit compares
means over 54 and 24 runs, so a difference below the bar is inside its own noise.
