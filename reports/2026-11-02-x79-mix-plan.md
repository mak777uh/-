# X79 — does the memory loss grow with the weight of the imposed second scale?

**Question.** X78b imposed a second scale as a 50/50 composite of 12.0 and 8.7 cells at `F = 0.030`, and the half-life fell from
1.47 to 1.03 (factor 1.43) while lag-1 stayed at 0.498. That is the direction the mixedness reading predicts, at about two thirds of
the size of the medium's own band effect (2.1–2.5×). Two explanations were left open: the imposed scale is a weaker perturbation
than two genuinely competing attractors, or the band's shallowness is partly about sitting near a phase boundary.

A sweep of the **weight** `--hetero-mix2` separates them: if memory keeps shortening as the second scale gets more weight, the
effect is graded in the perturbation and "imposed is weaker" is the explanation. If the effect **saturates** already at a small
weight, then a little second scale is enough and the band's extra shortening must come from something else (the phase boundary).

## 1. Manipulation is connected

The manipulation is `--hetero-mix2` (weight of the 8.7-cell component in `G`), with everything else fixed: `F = 0.030`, amp 0.5,
10 seeds, 6000 steps, 50-step logging. Connectedness is that the world must stay patterned (at least 10 components per world on
average, at least 400 lag-1 pairs per cell) and that the two arms must differ in their spectra: the arm with more weight on 8.7
cells must show a stronger peak near q ≈ 11 than the arm with less. If an arm collapses, it is void, not zero.

## 2. Positive control fires

The control fires if the **low-weight** arm reproduces the reference point of the sweep: with mix 0.25 the half-life must lie
**between** the single-scale 1.47 and the 50/50 value 1.03, i.e. in `(1.03, 1.47)`. A low-weight arm at 1.47 would mean the
manipulation has no effect at that weight (itself informative but it would make the sweep uninformative at the low end), and one at
≤1.03 would mean 0.25 already saturates.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

Same depth readout as X73–X78b: Spearman of ancestor on descendant peak area at lags 1–3, `λ`, half-life; plus the spectral check
from a field saved at 6000. The reference curve: 1.47 (mix 0), **1.03** (mix 0.5, X78b).

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

Two cells (mix 0.25 and 0.75), five worlds each, 6000 steps, 50-step logging, one field per run: ~90–110 s per run, so ~18 minutes.
Each cell should give ~700 lag-1 pairs; the Fisher band on `λ` is ~±0.1, so a half-life difference of 0.2 between cells is
detectable and a difference of 0.1 is not — stated in advance.

## 5. Refusal rule

**Void** for a cell that collapses (<10 components) or gives <400 lag-1 pairs.

**Graded effect** if the three points are monotone in weight — half-life(0.25) > half-life(0.5) > half-life(0.75) — with the 0.25-to-
0.75 difference at least 0.2 divisions.
**Saturating effect** if the two new cells differ from the 50/50 value by less than 0.15 and from each other by less than 0.1.
Anything else — out of order, or intermediate — is **inconclusive**, with all three points printed.
