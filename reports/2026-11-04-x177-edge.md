# X177 — The edge is not reachable: radius 4.5 never fuses at gap 11 (04.11)

**Question.** X175 left a two-factor picture and a band whose upper edge — the corridor at contact of the most marginal arm that
still merges — rested on three arms at share 0.27 (0.437, 0.492, 0.516) and on nothing more marginal. X176 tried to supply a more
marginal cell and overshot into "never fuses" (0 of 15 at radius 6, gap 10). This item tried the same thing at a size whose
crossing is one cell closer: **radius 4.5 at surface gap 11**.

Pre-registered: **share 0.05-0.30**, **corridor 0.50-0.56**.

## Result: 0 of 20, so there is nothing to read

| readout | predicted | observed |
|---|---|---|
| fusion share | 0.05-0.30 | **0.00** (0 of 20) |
| corridor at contact | 0.50-0.56 | **not measurable** (no contact event) |

X158 ran the same cell with fifteen seeds and saw 0 fusions, so radius 4.5 at gap 11 is now **0 of 35**. The registered rule
applies as written: no contact event, no corridor, and the item is recorded as **undecided** rather than as a confirmation or a
refutation.

## What this does to the two-factor picture and to the band

It leaves both where X176 left them, and it makes the reason structural rather than unlucky. Two attempts to reach the band's
upper edge have both failed for the same reason: **the arms one cell past a crossing do not fuse at all**, so the marginal-but-
alive cell the edge needs is not "one cell past the crossing" — the fusing range at each size is bounded above by a cell in which
the share is already about 0.3, and one cell further it is zero.

That turns the edge question into a different one. The upper edge of the band cannot be approached by making an arm more
marginal, because marginality and fusion are not both available at the same cell. It has to be approached by changing what
"marginal" means — the bridge's geometry (very short gaps with a colder source, where a merge would still happen) rather than the
distance between the bodies.

## Limits

Zero contact events: no corridor, no spread, no comparison. The registered corridor interval was not tested and must not be
counted as either hit or miss. The share interval was missed on the low side, which is the only quantitative statement here.

## What would kill it

A radius-4.5 run at gap 11 with a colder source (`F` = 0.026) or with jitter, i.e. a cell where fusion happens at all. If such a
run merges with a corridor in 0.50-0.56, the edge is reachable and the two failures above were about the arm's setup rather than
about the band.
