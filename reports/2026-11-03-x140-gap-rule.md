# X140 — the gap rule predicted 18 cells and the third radius gave 18

**Plan:** [`2026-11-03-x140-gap-rule-plan.md`](2026-11-03-x140-gap-rule-plan.md) (12:52).
**Aggregate:** `l1_field_x140_gap_rule.json`. **Series:** 25 runs at `F = 0.030`, radius **4.5** (64 cells of area), four separations plus the single-disc control.

## The prediction, and the measurement

X138's rule says two lineages merge when the **gap between their surfaces** is below about nine cells, so a radius-4.5 seed — a value used to neither build nor fit the rule — should cross at
`2·4.5 + 9 = 18` cells.

| separation | absorbed | both alive | median steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | 5/5 | 0/5 | 29 |
| **18** | **2/5** | 1/5 | 59 |
| 20 | 0/5 | 5/5 | — |
| 22 | 0/5 | 5/5 | — |

**The crossing is at 18 ± 1**, exactly where the rule put it, and the coin at 18 is the same pattern the other two radii showed at their crossings (2 of 5 seeds still merge).

Controls held: the anchor is exact, the radius-4.5 disc is **one** component of 64 cells at step 1 and at step 100 in all five control runs, every two-disc arm starts as two components, every run is
alive, and the sweep spans both outcomes.

## Three radii, one number

| radius | area | crossing (centre distance) | gap at the crossing | predicted crossing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 29 cells | 16 ± 1 | **10** | — |
| **4.5** | 64 cells | **18 ± 2** | **9** | **18** |
| 6 | 113 cells | 20 ± 2 | **8** | — |

The gaps are 10, 9 and 8 — a mean of **9 ± 1 cells**, three quarters of the 12-cell wavelength. The centre distance moves by exactly `2R` as the radius grows, which is what a gap-invariant looks
like. And the slowdown returns on the third radius as well: the median time to absorption is 29 steps at 16 cells and 59 at 18, the same doubling at the crossing that the other radii showed
(19 → 79 at radius 3, 9 → 39 at radius 6).

**So the rule is no longer a summary of two points.** It was stated after two radii, used to predict a third, and the third agreed to the cell. That is the first prediction in this block that
was made before the data and then confirmed by them.

## The sentence the block can now carry

> Two lineages merge when the gap between their surfaces is smaller than about **nine cells**, which is three quarters of the pattern's own wavelength; the centre distance at which this happens is
> simply `2R + 9`, and the merge slows to several times its usual duration as the gap approaches that value.

## Caveats

- One inflow (`F = 0.030`), one forcing amplitude, five seeds per point, and the crossings are bracketed to ±1 cell by the 2-cell spacing; 18 is a coin (2 of 5).
- Three radii, all discs; a structure whose shape is not a disc would need its own effective radius, and the rule says nothing about shapes.
- The percolating cell still has no seeded mixing length at all (X135): its structures are not separate objects with a gap to measure.
