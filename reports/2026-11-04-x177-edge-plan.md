# X177 — The band's upper edge: radius 4.5 at gap 11 (pre-registration, 04.11)

**Status:** pre-registered, not yet run.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

X175 left a two-factor picture — the corridor at contact rises with the arm's marginality and with the bridge's span — and X176
overshot when it tried to test it: radius 6 at gap 10 fused 0 of 15 (0 of 25 with X158), so there was no contact event and the
band's upper edge stayed untested. The edge now rests on three arms at share 0.27 (0.437, 0.492, 0.516) and nothing more
marginal.

This item supplies the marginal-but-alive cell the edge needs: **radius 4.5 at surface gap 11**. That size crosses at gap 10
(2/10 fusing in X158, 4 of 15 at gap 10 in X171), so at gap 11 it should fuse rarely but not never — and its bridge spans the
longest distance the block has run at this size.

## 2. Positive control fires

The control fires if the peak stays inside [0.05, 2.0] at every read (thresholds 0.05 and 2.0), the corridor reads below the
peak in **every** contact run (share 1.0), and the arm produces **at least two** contact events out of twenty — a lower bar than
X176's three, because the whole point of this cell is to sit near the floor of the fusing range. If zero runs fuse, the item is
undecided and says so; if one fuses, the single reading is reported as a case study and not as a median.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

X170's contact-time corridor, unchanged. With two or more contacts it must vary (sd ≥ 0.02); with one contact the readout is
reported without a spread.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic, and the prediction

Twenty seeds at radius 4.5, gap 11, `F` = 0.030. Registered **before the run**:

- **share: 0.05 to 0.30** (1 to 6 of 20), from this size's own ladder (0.80 at gap 9, 0.27 at 10) falling one more cell;
- **corridor: 0.50 to 0.56**, from the two-factor picture — more marginal than any arm measured, and spanning a longer bridge
  than the share-0.27 arms that read 0.437-0.516.

With two to four contacts the median's standard error is about 0.02-0.03, so a systematic offset of 0.05 is visible.

## 5. Refusal rule

If the corridor lands inside 0.50-0.56 with two or more contacts, the item is recorded as **the band's upper edge is real and
reachable**, and the band is 0.37-0.56 of the body peak. If it lands below 0.50, the edge is not where the two-factor picture
put it and the picture's gap factor is weaker than X175's single comparison suggested. If the share misses (more than 6 of 20
fuse), radius 4.5's crossing extends to gap 11 and the "one cell past the crossing" premise fails — reported as a failed
premise, not a failed prediction.

## 6. What would kill it

The marginal arms are exactly the ones with fewest contact events, so the upper edge of the band will always be measured on the
thinnest data. Twenty seeds give an expected one to six contacts; if it comes out at one, the item adds a case and not an edge,
and the honest conclusion will be that the band's upper edge cannot be established with the seeds this block is willing to spend.
