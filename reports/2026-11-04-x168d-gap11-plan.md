# X168d — Ten seeds at gap eleven: is the inflow trend real? (pre-registration, 04.11)

**Status:** pre-registered, not yet run.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

X168c found the crossing between ten and eleven cells at all three inflow rates and a gap-11 share that trends upward with
`F` (0/5 at 0.026, 1/5 at 0.030, 2/5 at 0.036). With five seeds that trend is exactly the size the design cannot resolve, and
the report said so. This item buys the resolution in the one cell where it matters: gap 11, the same three rates, **ten seeds
each** (961-970), 2 000 steps, separations 17. Nothing else changes, so the only difference from X168c is the seed count.

## 2. Positive control fires

The gap-8 arm must fuse in at least 9 of 10 runs at each rate (the control fires at 0.9, tighter than the 0.8 of the
five-seed version because ten seeds afford it). If a rate fails, its gap-11 arm is dropped and the report says why.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

The readout is the gap-11 fusion share per rate; it must not be 0/10 or 10/10 in all three rates (otherwise the readout is
saturated and cannot compare), and the gap at the last two-body step must vary (standard deviation ≥ 0.2 cells).

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

With ten seeds per rate, a share difference of 0.3 between two rates is detectable at about 80 % power (Fisher's exact test,
two-sided 0.05) when the intermediate rate sits near 0.5; the X168c point estimates are 0.0, 0.2 and 0.4, so the extreme
contrast (0.0 against 0.4) is detectable and the adjacent contrasts are not. That is the arithmetic this item is bought for,
and the report will state which contrasts clear it.

## 5. Refusal rule

If the three shares are flat and none of them differs from the X158 value at gap 11 (2/15 = 0.13) by more than the interval,
the item is recorded as **the trend was noise: the crossing does not depend on the inflow rate** and §4.1 keeps its
single-rate statement. If the extreme rates differ by 0.3 or more with a Fisher p below 0.05, the trend is real and the
crossing must be written as depending on the rate after all. If a rate's control fails, that rate is dropped rather than
averaged over.

## 6. What would kill it

Shares of 1/10, 2/10 and 4/10 with overlapping intervals would leave the question where X168c left it — and that is the most
likely outcome, since the point estimates are small. A monotone ladder that clears the extreme contrast would overturn the
"insensitive to the source" statement; a flat 0-2/10 in all three would confirm it, and this time with enough seeds to say
"no trend" rather than "cannot see one".

## 7. Implementation notes

Runner `scripts/x168d_series.py` (three rates × ten seeds at gap 11, plus a gap-8 control at each rate, 60 runs of 2 000
steps), analysis `scripts/x168d_analyse.py` (shares with Wilson intervals, Fisher's exact tests between the extreme rates,
comparison with the X158 baseline). Aggregate
`morphogenesis/experiments/E09_pond/l1_field_x168d_gap11.json`. Cost about 30 minutes.
