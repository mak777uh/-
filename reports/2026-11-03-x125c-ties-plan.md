# X125c — the tie rule is the last undeclared filter: is X93's fraction bare once ties are counted?

**Why.** X125b showed that X93's `within_fraction` (0.530 at `F = 0.030`, 0.401 at 0.040) is computed with **ties excluded** — `x93_analyse.py` skips a pair when
the sisters' lifetimes are equal — while the same fraction over all pairs on X108 reads 0.409 and 0.305. With the median pair difference at exactly zero, ties are
common, so the tie rule alone could account for the whole gap. This run asks that question directly, and nothing else.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

X108's twenty dense runs, the sister pairs of X97/X113/X124/X125b. No new worlds, no parameters, one rule changed and named:

| statistic | definition | filter |
|---|---|---|
| **tie share** `t` | fraction of pairs with identical generation times (logged steps) | none |
| **fraction larger, tie-excluded** `p_nt` | among pairs with different generation times, the fraction in which the larger sister's is longer | ties removed (**X93's rule**) |
| **fraction larger, all pairs** `p_all` | ties counted in the denominator as "not larger" | none (X125b's rule) |
| **mean difference, tie-excluded** | mean of `g_big − g_small` over non-tied pairs | ties removed |

Connectedness: the same pairs that produced X125b's 1 142 and 1 195 pairs; the tie share converts one fraction into the other, so `p_all = p_nt × (1 − t)` must
hold and is checked as an arithmetic identity, not assumed.

## 2. Positive control fires (numeric)

Three controls, all required; the control fires if all three hold:

1. **Heredity** `≥ 0.35` in both cells (0.548 / 0.480).
2. **Depth contrast** reproduces X113 (+1.479 / +2.213) within 10 % — the statistic that no filter has moved yet; if it moves here, the tie rule is not local to
   the fraction and the whole block is suspect.
3. **The identity holds arithmetically:** `p_all` recomputed as `p_nt × (1 − t)` must equal the counted `p_all` to within 1e-9. This is the control that the two
   fractions are the same quantity under two rules, which is the point of the run.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

Per cell: `t`, `p_nt` with a bootstrap 95 % interval, `p_all`, `n_non_tied`, and the tie-excluded mean difference with its interval. Variation is not in question:
`p_nt` is 0.53 by X93's own measurement and 0.409 is known to be different; the run's question is whether the difference is the tie rule.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

Ties are expected to be a large share (X125b's median is zero), so the usable count is `n × (1 − t)`. At `t = 0.5` and `n = 1 142` that is 571 pairs; a proportion
there has SE = √(0.25/571) = 0.021, so the 95 % interval is ±0.041. That is **wide enough to bracket X93's 0.530** (which is the question) and too wide to resolve
0.53 from 0.50 at 2 σ (which is not the question). The declared purpose is therefore an **agreement check**, and the plan says so before the numbers: this run can
retire the contradiction or keep it, but it cannot establish the fraction on X108.

## 5. Refusal rule (fixed before the run)

**Void** if a cell has fewer than 300 non-tied pairs, or heredity < 0.35, or the depth control misses its 10 % band, or the arithmetic identity of §2 misses 1e-9.

**The contradiction is retired** if X93's value lies inside the `p_nt` interval in both cells and the tie share explains the gap in sign as well as in size (i.e.
`p_nt > p_all` in both).
**The contradiction stands** if the `p_nt` interval excludes X93's value in either cell — then the two series genuinely disagree about the direction of the
generation-time contrast, the disagreement is a property of the worlds rather than of the vocabulary, and it is recorded as such in `docs/eliminations.md`.
