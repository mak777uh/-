## It is a crossover, not a wall

The plan said that a clean 3-of-5 boundary keeps the word "wall" and that a smooth rise drops it. The data give **neither extreme**: the boundary is at one cell — 5/5 up to 15, **2/5 at 16**, 0/5 from 17 — and inside that cell the outcome is already mixed (2 of 5 seeds still merge, 1 of 5 keeps both lineages). So the mixing length is

> **16 cells, with a one-cell transition width (15 merges, 16 is a coin, 17 does not).**

**And the merge slows as it approaches.** The median time to absorption rises monotonically with separation — 19, 39, 49, 79 steps at 12, 14, 15, 16 — which is what a critical approach looks like: just inside the mixing length the two structures take five times as long to become one as they do well inside it. That is a second, independent signature of a threshold, and it was not in the plan: the plan measured whether a merge happens, not how long it takes.

The wavelength is 12 cells, so the mixing length is **about 1.3 wavelengths** — not one, as X134's coarse bracket suggested, and not the "exactly one" that the first (faulty) sweep seemed to confirm.

## What this settles

The block's mechanism — *who is within one mixing length of whom* — now has a number with a one-cell bracket and a second signature. Combined with the observational identity block, the line can say: two unrelated lineages at this inflow merge if they start within **16 ± 1 cells**; the pattern's own wavelength is 12 cells, so the mixing length sits a third of a wavelength above it; and the merge itself slows to a crawl at the edge of that range.

## Caveats

- Five seeds per point and a three-of-five rule: 16 cells is called a coin because 2 of 5 merged, and with five seeds that is not distinguishable from 1 or 3 of 5.
- One inflow, one radius (3 cells), one amplitude of forcing. The maze cannot be swept this way at all (X135: the forcing overwrites the seeds).
- The merge time is measured to the first logged step at which one seeded uid ends into the other's reach; a genuinely slower merge beyond 6 000 steps would be censored as "not merged", which would move the crossover **outward**, not inward.
