# X151 — is the stop a threshold or a plateau? Radius 5.25 at gaps 7 and 8

**Why.** X150 measured the boundary of universal coalescence at three radii and found it moving inward once and then
stopping: radius 3 holds 5/5 out to a surface gap of 8 cells, radius 4.5 only to 7, radius 6 also to 7. Two radii agree
on the far side of the move and one disagrees, so the shape of the stop is unknown in two ways that matter:

- a **threshold** at a size between 28 and 64 cells of disc area — the boundary jumps to 7 as soon as the disc is big
  enough, and stays there;
- a **plateau** — the boundary would keep creeping inward with the radius but the sweep's one-cell spacing cannot see it,
  and radius 6 lands on 7 only because the next cell (6) is not measured.

Radius 5.25 (area 87 cells, between the two that disagree) separates them: at gap 8 it is either 5/5 (the plateau was
reached by 4.5, the stop is a threshold crossed before 64 cells) or mixed (the creep continues and radius 6's 5/5 at gap
7 is the last cell before the boundary).

## 1. Manipulation is connected

Same probe (`--init-spots R:SEP`), same inflow `F = 0.030`, same seeds 601–605, same 6 000 steps, dense logging every
10 steps, the same tolerance-free readout. The manipulation is the surface gap at a radius that sits **between** the two
radii which disagree, so the point of the run is a comparison the sweep could not make before.

| arm | radius | gap | separation | runs |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `x151_g7_r525` | 5.25 | 7 | 17.5 | 5 new |
| `x151_g8_r525` | 5.25 | 8 | 18.5 | 5 new |

## 2. Positive control fires (numeric)

Four controls. **The control fires if** all four hold:

1. **The timing law repeats.** Median time to mutual coalescence in whichever arm is 5/5 M must be **39** logged steps
   at gap 7 and **49** at gap 8 — the numbers all three earlier radii gave. This is the check that the new radius is the
   same world; it does not depend on the boundary question.
2. **Every arm starts as two components** of area ≥ 20 cells at the first logged step (a radius-5.25 disc is about 87
   cells, so the pair is about 174).
3. **No arm ends empty** (occupancy ≥ 5 % of the grid at the last step).
4. **Medians are printed with their n**, and the number of coalescing seeds is stated per arm.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

The class spans all three values in the neighbouring arms, and the two arms of this run sit exactly where the transition
is expected, so the readout has room to vary within the sweep. Half-integer separation is not a new kind of measurement:
the probe places discs at centres that need not be integers, and X142's control verified that the placement depends only
on the seed.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

Five seeds per arm, two arms: the resolution is one seed and half a cell of separation. The declared minimal effect is
the difference between "5/5 M at gap 8" and "at most 3/5 M", two seeds — the same yardstick that separated radius 3 from
radius 4.5 and radius 6.

## 5. Refusal rule (fixed before the run)

**Void** if control 1 fails (the timing law must repeat, otherwise the radius is not the same world), if an arm does not
start as two components, or if an arm ends empty.

The registered outcomes, all of which decide something:

- **Threshold, crossed before 64 cells**: radius 5.25 is 5/5 M at gap 8. Then the boundary has already stopped by 87
  cells, radius 6's 5/5 at gap 7 is the plateau, and the stop lies between 28 and 64 cells of disc area.
- **The creep continues**: radius 5.25 is at most 3/5 M at gap 8. Then the boundary is still moving at 87 cells, the
  agreement of 4.5 and 6 at gap 7 is not a plateau, and the next run is radius 5.25 at gap 7 — if that is also mixed,
  the boundary moves by less than a cell and the sweep needs sub-cell gaps rather than more radii.
- **The boundary is not monotone in the radius**: radius 5.25 is 5/5 at gap 8 **and** mixed at gap 7. Then the boundary
  went out and back between 4.5, 5.25 and 6, which contradicts X147 and X150 and means the arms are not measuring one
  quantity; the report must say so and the block stops to re-examine the readout before any further radius.
- **Indeterminate**: radius 5.25 gap 8 is exactly 4/5 M. Then one seed separates the arms; the next run doubles the
  seeds at gaps 7 and 8 rather than adding a radius.
