# X128b — the local budget: isolated, absorbed, vanished, and only then who takes the cells

**Why.** [X128 voided](2026-11-03-x128-kinship-void.md) and took X127's interpretation with it: both used a **world-level** area budget to classify a **local** event,
so in a world of twenty isolated spots "absorbed" meant "something somewhere grew". This amendment restricts the budget to the dead structure's own neighbourhood,
adds the category the lattice needs — a death with **no live neighbour at all** — and only then asks the kinship question again.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

No new runs: the same thirty dense logs (X108 at `0.030`/`0.040`, X126 at `0.035`). The manipulation is the inflow; the instrument is now local.

| quantity | definition |
|---|---|
| **neighbourhood** | survivors whose centroid is within `r_dead + r_survivor + 2` cells of the dead component's centroid, with `r = sqrt(area/π)` |
| **isolated** | the neighbourhood is empty |
| **absorbed** | local growth (only the neighbours' area change) ≥ 50 % of the dead uid's area |
| **vanished** | local growth ≤ 10 % of the dead uid's area |
| **unclear** | between the thresholds, reported with its share |
| **absorber** | among the neighbours, the one whose area grew most, provided it holds ≥ 50 % of the local positive growth; otherwise the absorption is **diffuse** |

Connectedness: every death in every world receives exactly one of the four fates, the four counts sum to the death count, and the neighbourhood size is reported.

## 2. Positive control fires (numeric)

Five controls, all required. **The control fires if** all five hold:

1. **End-survival reproduces X126** to four decimals (+0.2496 / +0.2984 / +0.1372) with pair counts 1 142 / 1 106 / 1 195.
2. **Heredity** `ρ₁ ≥ 0.35` (0.548 / 0.421 / 0.480).
3. **The local budget closes**: for every death, the neighbours' total area change equals the sum of their individual changes within 1 cell, and the change of the
   **whole world** equals the sum of local changes plus the change contributed by non-neighbours within 2 cells. This is the control that the local and global
   accounts are two views of the same step, not two different datasets.
4. **Coverage is total**: isolated + absorbed + vanished + unclear = deaths, per cell.
5. **The old and new instruments disagree in the predicted direction**: the lattice's absorbed share must **drop** by at least 0.20 against X127's 84.5 %. If it
   does not, the neighbourhood restriction is not doing anything and the diagnosis of the void is wrong.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

Per cell: the four shares, the median neighbourhood size, the median dead area, and — among the absorbed with a partner — the kinship shares from X128's definitions
(sibling / aunt / cousin / distant / unrelated). Variation is guaranteed by the geometry: the lattice's spots are separated by more than a structure width, the
maze's are one body.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

Deaths per cell 524 / 844 / 1 102 (X127). If the lattice's isolated share is as large as the void suggests, the absorbed count there may fall to ~100 — which is the
floor, so the lattice's kinship census may become **void by count** while the maze's stays. That is declared now: the kinship question is answerable in the
percolating cells and may not be answerable in the lattice at this grid, and in that case only the fate census is published.
Declared resolution: **0.08** in a share over ≥ 300 deaths (SE 0.029), and 0.15 over ≥ 100 (SE 0.05).

## 5. Refusal rule (fixed before the run)

**Void** if heredity < 0.35, or end-survival misses X126 by more than 0.01, or the local/global accounts disagree on more than 1 % of steps, or the fate counts do
not sum to the deaths.

**The fate census stands** if the four shares are reported with their intervals and the lattice's absorbed share falls by ≥ 0.20 against X127, whatever the maze
does. **The kinship question is answered** only for a cell with ≥ 100 partnered absorptions; a cell below that is reported as "not answerable at this grid" and is
**not** merged with the others.
**No registered outcome** is again a permitted verdict and must be written in those words.
