# X82 — how far can inflow modulation move the spectrum? (the reach of the manipulation, measured before any heredity claim)

**Why this exists, and why it replaces X81 as registered.** X81 was going to prescribe a second spectral peak at shares 0 / 0.5 / 1.0
and read heredity off the measured share. Three pilot runs at 6000 steps, seed 181, `F = 0.030` (not part of this series; they are
the reason for it) show that the manipulation **does not reach**:

| prescribed power ratio | realised share in the field | components | lag-1 pairs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | **0.19** | 37 | 76 splits |
| 4.0 | **0.31** | 37 | 76 splits |
| 16.0 | **0.28** | 29 | 69 splits |

Prescribing sixteen times the power of the second peak buys a shoulder of about 0.3. The medium keeps its own preferred scale and
admits the imposed one only as a minority. That is why X78's matched arm collapsed, why X79's weights changed almost nothing in the
field, and why X80's three cells turned out to be spectrally near-identical when the share was measured instead of a local maximum
counted (`reports/2026-11-02-x80-correction-two-peak.md`).

So the question "does mixedness shorten memory" cannot be asked through this channel: the channel has a ceiling, and the ceiling is
below anything that would make the words "equal parts" true. The question that *can* be answered, and is worth an answer, is how the
channel behaves — because every future mixedness design has to be built on that number.

**This series measures the transfer function `prescribed share → realised share`, with worlds.**

## 1. Manipulation is connected

Five cells differing only in `--spectrum-share` (and one in `--hetero-scale2`). The realised share is computed from the saved field
at 6000 with the definition used in the X80 correction, fixed here before the run:

- dominant family: the local maximum `q1*` of the radial power profile in bins **[6, 9]**; `P1 = Σ P(k)` over `|k − q1*| <= 1`;
- second family: the local maximum `q2*` in bins **[10, 13]** for the q = 11 cells, and in **[13, 18]** for the q = 14 cell;
  `P2 = Σ P(k)` over `|k − q2*| <= 1`;
- realised share `= P2 / (P1 + P2)`, one number per world.

Connectedness is the monotonicity of that number against the prescribed ratio; the check is not a peak count, because a peak count
passed on a tie in X80 and hid the collapse.

## 2. Positive control fires (numeric)

The `share = 0` cell is the single-scale world at this inflow. **The control fires if** its median realised share is **below 0.15**
and its pooled lag-1 correlation lies in **[0.45, 0.56]** (X74's single-scale 0.030 cell: 0.507). If the single-scale world already
shows a second peak above 0.15, the "share" measure is picking the medium's own harmonics and the transfer function is not readable.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

`rho1` and the depth readout (lags 1–3, per-world `lambda`, median aggregation) are recorded for every world so the same fields can
serve the heredity question later; but the **primary readout of X82 is the realised share**, and it is expected to vary by a factor
of two across cells (0.0 → 0.3).

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

Realised share per world: sd ≈ 0.02–0.05 (X80 cells: 0.18–0.23 within a cell). Five worlds per cell → SE ≈ 0.01–0.02, so a
difference of **0.05** between cells is 2.5–5σ and is the declared resolution. The plateau is declared if
`share(16) − share(4) < 0.05` while `share(4) − share(1) >= 0.05`. Cost: 25 runs at ~95 s ≈ **40 minutes**.

## 5. Refusal rule

**Void** if a cell has <10 components or <400 lag-1 pairs, or if the `share = 0` control fails §2, or if the profile has no local
maximum in the second window (which would mean the imposed peak is absent rather than weak).

**Saturating** if `share(1) < share(4)` by at least 0.05 and `share(16) − share(4) < 0.05`.
**Graded** if `share(16)` exceeds `share(4)` by at least 0.05 — then the ceiling has not been reached and the shares have to be
pushed further before any mixedness design is built.
**Wavelength-dependent** if the q = 14 cell at prescribed 4 differs from the q = 11 cell at prescribed 4 by at least 0.05.
Anything else is **inconclusive**, printed in full.

## What this buys

A number that every later design must respect: the strongest second peak this channel can deliver at `F = 0.030`, and whether it
depends on the imposed wavelength. If the ceiling is 0.3, then "mixedness" in this world means *a preferred scale plus a 30 %
shoulder*, and any claim about mixtures must be phrased that way — which is a much sharper statement than "the sweep was
inconclusive", and it is the sentence that tells us whether two-scale media are reachable here at all.
