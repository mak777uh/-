# X114 — the generation advantage survives an equal observation window

**Plan:** [`2026-11-03-x114-window-plan.md`](2026-11-03-x114-window-plan.md) (00:52).
**Aggregate:** `l1_field_x114_window.json`. Analysis of X108's dense series — **regenerated** from its seeds tonight, and checked against the committed
aggregates to the digit (see "Data restored" below).

## The test

X113 and X113c measured depth over the whole 6 000-step run, so a line that starts late or dies early has less time to go deep, and lines alive at the
horizon are favoured. X114 gives both branches of a sister pair the **same budget**: generations reached inside `W = 3 000` steps of each branch's own
first appearance, counting only descendants born inside that window.

| cell | sister pairs | heredity (control) | median depth: windowed vs whole-run | rate contrast (generations per 1 000 windowed steps) | 95 % CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.030 | 1 142 | 0.548 ✓ | **2 vs 3** | **+0.407** | **[0.362, 0.453]** |
| 0.040 | 1 195 | 0.480 ✓ | **2 vs 3** | **+0.638** | **[0.561, 0.720]** |

Registered verdict: **the advantage survives the equal window** — both intervals exclude zero, and both controls fired: heredity is unchanged
(0.548 / 0.480), and the window demonstrably bites (median depth falls from 3 to 2 in both cells, so this is not the previous measurement in
disguise).

The magnitudes are **not** comparable with X113c's +6.06 / +18.96: that rate divides each branch's depth by **its own lifetime**, while this one divides
the depth *difference* by the fixed window. Two denominators, one question — and the answer is the same sign and the same conclusion.

## What this settles

The caveat carried by the day's consequence claim — "depth is a count within a run, so truncation favours lines still alive at the end" — is now
tested and does not explain the effect. With equal windows the larger sister's line still reaches more generations per unit time, in both cells.

What it does not settle: whether reaching generations is *worth* anything for persistence, and whether the window itself (3 000 of 6 000 steps, with
about 450 pairs per cell whose window reaches past the series end) is the right horizon. That is a design choice, registered here so it can be
attacked.

## Caveats

- The 3 000-step window is half the run; a different `W` would change the magnitudes, though not the sign (the sign was already robust to using each
  branch's own lifetime).
- About 450 pairs per cell have a window that runs past the series end, where the window is nominal rather than exact; the rest are exact.
- Rate contrasts from the two denominators (own lifetime, fixed window) must not be compared in size.
- One seed set, one box, one grid; areas at the 0.20 cut.
