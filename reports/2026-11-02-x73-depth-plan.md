# X73 — how deep is the size memory? Ancestor–descendant decay over the genealogical distance

**Question.** X70 measured one step: a daughter's peak area follows her own parent's, at about half strength (0.42–0.57 over
five worlds), and the effect survives a nearest-distance surrogate on the same events (0.457 against 0.069, 10/10 worlds).
That says the memory lasts **one division**. It does not say what happens at the second: does a granddaughter resemble her
grandparent, or does the medium erase the deviation in one step and then put everything back to its preferred scale?

The answer decides which class of heredity this is. If the correlation at lag 1 is 0.45 and at lag 2 is ~0.2, the memory is
an exponentially decaying one and a lineage is a random walk around the medium's scale — "limited heredity" in Szathmáry's
sense, with a measurable half-life. If lag 2 is ~0, the medium resets every generation and there is no lineage-level state at
all.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

No manipulation; this is a re-analysis of the genealogies that already exist in `x72c_pattern_s171-180.json` (and, as a
replication, in `x70r_pattern_s171-175.json`). Connectedness is the identity link: `uid_pairs` must contain chains of length
≥ 2 — parent → child → grandchild — in enough worlds to measure. If chains are too rare, the series is void by construction,
not negative.

## 2. Positive control fires

Two numbers, both before the decay curve is interpreted.

1. **Lag-1 reproduces X70:** pooled Spearman at lag 1 must be **≥ 0.30** across the X72c events. If it is not, the genealogy
   reconstruction is wrong (or the sample changed) and nothing at lag ≥ 2 can be believed.
2. **The surrogate is weak:** at lag 1 the nearest non-parent at the daughter's birth must give **≤ 0.15**, the value X70's
   control measured (0.069). A surrogate as strong as the parent would mean the distance control is not doing its job here.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

For every uid with a recorded peak area, the ancestor at lag `k` is reached by following `uid_pairs` upward `k` times. The
statistic is Spearman(ancestor peak, descendant peak) over all pairs at that lag, computed per world and pooled, for
`k = 1, 2, 3`.

Two controls at every lag:

- **spatial:** for each descendant, the nearest structure at its birth that is **not** an ancestor of any order (a stranger),
  and the same correlation with that stranger's peak;
- **lineage:** a shuffle that pairs each descendant with a lag-`k` ancestor drawn from a **different** lineage in the same
  world, 2000 draws, giving the p95 of the null.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

The X72c worlds hold 1 955 division events in ten worlds; lag-1 pairs ≈ 1 900, lag-2 ≈ 700–900 (a chain needs both links
observed), lag-3 ≈ 250–350 by the same geometric argument. With `n = 300` the Fisher band for `rho = 0.2` is about ±0.11, so
lag 3 can distinguish "2000-ish memory" from "0" but not from 0.3; that is stated in advance rather than discovered afterwards.
No GPU: this is a re-analysis, roughly 20 minutes of work.

The decay is reported as three numbers per lag (parent, stranger, shuffled p95) and as a fitted half-life only if the three lags
are monotone; a non-monotone curve is reported as noise, not fitted.

## 5. Refusal rule

**Void** if fewer than 100 lag-2 pairs exist in the pooled data, or if control 1 fails.

**Depth ≥ 2** if pooled lag-2 `rho ≥ 0.15` and it exceeds the shuffled p95 and the stranger in ≥ 7 of 10 worlds.
**Depth 1 only** if lag-2 `rho < 0.10` in the pooled estimate **and** in ≥ 7 of 10 worlds — the medium resets in one generation.
Anything between 0.10 and 0.15 is **inconclusive**, reported as such with the numbers.
