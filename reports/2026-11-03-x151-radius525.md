## Correction 1 — my registration's branch labels were wrong, and the numbers fired the branch I mis-described

I registered two outcomes for the gap-8 arm: "5/5 → a threshold crossed before 64 cells" and "at most 3/5 → the creep
continues". The measurement is 3/5, so the second branch's *numbers* fired — but its *interpretation* was written wrong.
If radius 4.5 (64 cells), 5.25 (87) and 6 (113) all break at the same gap, the boundary is not still moving at 87 cells;
it has stopped. The branch label carried a story that contradicted its own numbers, and the numbers are what count.

The plan is amended below rather than rewritten. The lesson is about how I write registrations, not about the world: a
branch must be labelled by the number it predicts and its reading, never by the story I expect to tell.

## Correction 2 — "at gap 8 all three radii dissolve the pair at 49 steps" was a median, and at radius ≥ 4.5 one seed is late

The X150 report said the pair is one body by step 49 at gap 8 on all three radii. Per seed it is not:

| radius | coalescing seeds at gap 8, their steps |
|---|---|
| 3.0 | 49, 49, 49, 49, 49 |
| 4.5 | 49, 49, 49 |
| 5.25 | 49, 49, **59** |
| 6.0 | 49, 49, **59** |

The **median** is 49 at every radius, which is what was reported, but one seed in three is ten steps late at radius 4.5 and
above. At gaps 6 and 7 the law is exact per seed — twenty seed-runs across four radii, every one at 29 or 39 with no
exception — so the honest statement is: **while coalescence is universal the time is the gap's to the step; at the gap
where the boundary is crossed the median still is, but the distribution grows a tail.**

The X150 report carries a dated correction note; its CLAIMS row is tightened in the same commit.

## What the block now says

1. **Timing**: 29 steps at gap 6, 39 at gap 7, 49 at gap 8 (median), and 49 / 39 identical per seed while coalescence is
   universal. Independent of the radius over a 4× range of disc area.
2. **Outcome**: universal out to a gap of 8 cells at 28 cells of disc area, out to 7 at 64, 87 and 113 — the boundary is
   a step whose position is set by something that saturates between 28 and 64 cells.
3. **Where they meet**: crossing the boundary leaves the median time alone and adds a ten-step tail to a third of the
   seeds. That is the first sign that the two laws touch at all.

## Caveats

- The plateau is measured at three sizes (64, 87, 113 cells) and the step is bracketed by two (28 and 64). Nothing here
  locates the step, and nothing says the boundary at gap 7 does not creep *within* the cell — a sub-cell gap at radius 6
  (separation 19.5) would say.
- One seed per arm decides the difference between "3/5" and "5/5", so the plateau's width is what is solid, not its edge.
- Disc areas are geometric (πR²), not measured.
