# X77 — is the size memory parental, or just the local patch?

**Question.** X70's spatial control compared a daughter with the **nearest non-parent** structure, and the parent won (0.457
against 0.069, 10/10 worlds). That is a one-neighbour control, and it is the weakest form of the objection: what matters is not
the nearest structure but the **patch** — everything within about one wavelength of the daughter's birth — which sets her size,
with the parent merely one member of it. If the patch mean explains the daughter's size and the parent adds nothing once the patch
is in the model, then "size heredity" is a restatement of "the daughter sits where the parent sat", which is exactly what killed
the branch-heredity claim (X72c).

This is the sharpest remaining test of the line's central positive result, and it needs no new runs.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

No manipulation; re-analysis of the division events already in `x72c_pattern_s171-180.json` (primary) and
`x70r_pattern_s171-175.json` (replication). Connectedness is that the patch control has something to control with: the patch mean
must correlate with the daughter's peak area at **ρ ≥ 0.20**. A patch mean that predicts nothing means the control is vacuous and
the parent's survival is uninformative.

## 2. Positive control fires

Three numbers, before the parent's coefficient is read.

1. **Patch has power:** `ρ(patch_mean, daughter) ≥ 0.20` in the primary dataset.
2. **Enough events:** at least 1 000 events with a non-empty patch, and a median patch of at least 3 structures within the radius.
3. **The reference control reproduces X70:** `ρ(nearest non-parent, daughter)` must be in `[0.00, 0.20]`, the range X70's control
   measured (0.069). Outside it, this event set is not comparable with X70 and the comparison is void.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

Per division event, at the daughter's birth snapshot: the parent's peak area, the daughter's peak area, and the mean peak area of
all structures within **12 cells** (about one wavelength at `F = 0.035`) of the daughter's centroid that are **not ancestors** of
her and not herself. The statistic is the coefficient of the parent in
`daughter ~ parent + patch_mean` (OLS), with a bootstrap CI over 2 000 resamples of events, and the two marginal Spearman
correlations for context.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic

X72c has 1 955 events and X70r 1 034; both are far above the 1 000 bar. The bootstrap band on a coefficient with `n ≈ 2 000` is
tens of times tighter than the effect sizes in play, so the test is limited by whether the patch absorbs the parent, not by
sample size. Runtime is a couple of minutes — the fields are not needed, only the component log.

## 5. Refusal rule

**Void** if control 1 or 3 fails (no power, or the reference control does not reproduce X70).

**Parent-specific beyond the patch** if the bootstrap 95 % CI of the parent's coefficient lies entirely **above zero** in the
primary dataset **and** in the replication.
**Not separable** if the CI includes zero in the primary dataset.
Anything else — one dataset passing, one not — is **inconclusive**, with both tables printed.
