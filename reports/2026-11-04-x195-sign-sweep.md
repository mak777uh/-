# X195 — where does the sign change? (04.11)

**Question.** X193 (radius 3 gap 10) and X192 (radius 6 gap 9) find the same mechanism — at a fixed local inflow the bridge decides
the outcome — with **opposite** signs of the inflow–outcome relation: the fusion share rises with the inflow at radius 3 (Spearman
+0.413 over classes, +0.515 over value means) and falls at radius 6 (−0.378, −0.833). The bridge account explains the reversal as a
size effect, and that account predicts the sign must move monotonically somewhere between the two. This item runs the same phase
census at **radius 4 gap 12** and **radius 5 gap 10**, both with a centre separation of 20 cells, so the inflow band and the phase
map stay as close to the measured arms as the geometry allows. Preregistration
`reports/2026-11-04-x195-sign-sweep-plan.md`.

**Result: there is a window of sizes with no fusion at all — the sign is not defined there.**

| 4 (12, 20) | 144/144 | 0 | 0.13926 (44 classes) | -0.686 / -0.939 | 0.0–0.0 |
| 5 (10, 20) | 144/144 | 0 | 0.17997 (44 classes) | -0.492 / -0.43 | 0.0–0.0 |

Registered criteria: **one passes, one is not testable, one fails.**
1. **the phase decides the bridge at fixed inflow in each arm — PASSES.** The spread at the most crowded value is
   0.13926 (radius 4) and 0.17997 (radius 5) against a bar of 0.05, at 44
   classes each.
2. **the outcome follows the bridge at fixed inflow in each arm — NOT TESTABLE, not failed.** Not one of the 144 radius-4 worlds or the 144 radius-5
   worlds that have a measurable bridge ever reaches one component: **fused worlds 0 and 0**. With no variation in the outcome there is
   no contrast to read and no logistic slope (the fit is undefined), so this criterion is recorded as untestable rather than missed.
3. **the sign moves monotonically with size — FAILS.** [0.413, -0.686, -0.492, -0.378] over classes and [0.515, -0.939, -0.43, -0.833] over the value means, not non-increasing; the
   only crossing is between radius 3 and radius 4.

**What this says about X193's phrasing.** X193 concluded "one mechanism, and size sets its sign". The bridge side of that survives here
— the ratio still falls with the inflow in the radius-6 direction (-0.686 and
-0.492 over classes) and the phase still decides it — but the **outcome** side does not interpolate:
across size the fusion outcome is non-monotone in *existence*, present at radius 3, absent at radius 4 and 5, partly present again at
radius 6. So "size sets the sign" holds for the two measured arms, and between them lies a window of sizes where a touched pair never
coalesces and the sign is undefined. That is a restriction of X193's conclusion, and it is stated as one.

**Why this matters for the law.** The law of local inflow says that a weaker inflow fuses less. At separations near 20 cells with
intermediate bodies the answer is that **nothing fuses**, whatever the inflow: the mechanism's outcome term has a size window, not
just a sign. A follow-up that varied the separation at fixed radius would separate "the bodies are too large for this gap" from "this
radius is special" — and the radius-6 arm's own partial fusion (see X192 and X190) suggests the gap is the binding variable, since
radius 6 with gap 9 fuses while radius 4 and 5 with a 20-cell separation do not.

**Limits.** Both arms are one representative world per phase class, at one nominal inflow, with the separation held at 20 cells, so
"no fusion" is a statement about this geometry band. The class unit rests on X190/X191's zero within-site and within-phase spread and
on X194's finding that the selected arms are where the clone structure lives. And the absence of an outcome is not an absence of
dynamics: the bridge was measurable in all 288 worlds, so the arms are informative about the bridge and silent about the outcome.

**Next hypotheses.** (1) Vary the **gap at fixed radius 4** (12 → 14 → 16 at radius 4, and 10 → 13 → 16 at radius 5) to find whether
the window closes by bringing the bodies closer, which distinguishes "too far apart" from "wrong size". (2) The same census at
radius 6 with a 20-cell separation, to check whether radius 6 fuses because of its size or because of its 21-cell gap — the two
explanations are separable by one arm. (3) Two seeds per class at radius 3, where the outcome does vary, to price the class unit.
