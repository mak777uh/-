# X132 — the power run: thirty worlds per cell, because ten could not resolve the survival contrast

**Why.** X131 tested every cell difference at the world level and found that the one **the whole consequence chain rests on** — the larger sister's lineage survives
more often in the lattice than in the maze, +0.2496 against +0.1372 — is **not resolved** by ten worlds: as a paired per-seed difference it is −0.077 with an
interval of −0.166…+0.012, and the world-to-world spread (sd 0.14) is the same size as the effect. That is a **power** problem with a computable price: a paired
test over `n` worlds resolves a difference `d` at 80 % power when `d / (sd/√n) ≥ 2.8`, i.e. `n ≥ (2.8·0.14/0.077)² ≈ 26`. Thirty worlds per cell is that number
rounded up, and it also gives the depth contrast (+1.049, sd 0.69–1.67) a fair test.

This is the first experiment in the block whose **size is set by a computed requirement** rather than by what ran before.

## 1. Manipulation is connected

Twenty **new** worlds per cell at the two extreme inflows (`F = 0.030` and `0.040`), seeds **311–330**, added to the existing ten (301–310) so each cell has thirty.
Flags byte-identical to `x108_series.py` and `x126_series.py` (dense logging every 10 steps, `--split-new-uid`, no field saving, whose inertness X126's anchor
proved key-by-key against `x108_f030_s301.json`). The anchor is re-run for this series with the same comparison before the twenty start.

The readouts are unchanged, and each is computed **per world** first:

| readout | definition |
|---|---|
| end-survival contrast | X126's readout, mean over the world's sister pairs |
| depth contrast | X113's readout, mean over the world's sister pairs |
| vanished / absorbed / kin shares | X128b's local budget and partner rule, over the world's deaths |

## 2. Positive control fires (numeric)

Five controls. **The control fires if** all five hold:

1. **The anchor is exact**: `F = 0.030`, seed 301, this script's flags, against `x108_f030_s301.json`, every key identical except the two declared field-saving
   entries, `uid_pairs` identical.
2. **The ten old worlds are unchanged**: their per-world values reproduce X131's published per-world table exactly (same logs, so this is a self-check of the
   analysis code, not of the data).
3. **Structural liveness** in all forty new runs (occupancy ≥ 0.05, largest component ≥ 50 cells).
4. **Heredity** `ρ₁ ≥ 0.35` in each cell, now over thirty worlds.
5. **Coverage**: every new world contributes at least one sister pair and at least twenty deaths; a world that contributes nothing is reported, not dropped.

## 3. Readout exists and varies

Per cell and per readout: thirty per-world values (printed in full), the pooled value, the world-level sd, and the pooled and world-level intervals. The paired
per-seed difference between the cells over **thirty** seeds is the primary statistic, with its bootstrap interval.

## 4. Sensitivity arithmetic (this is the design)

sd across worlds taken from X131: survival 0.1377 (lattice) and 0.0941 (maze), depth 0.6896 and 1.6665. Pairing is by seed but the seeds are new, so the paired
difference's spread is taken to be at least as large as the larger of the two sds: 0.14 for survival.

- Survival: 30 worlds → SE 0.026 → the observed 0.077 is 3.0 SE. **Resolvable.**
- If the true difference is 0.05, the same design gives 1.9 SE and the run will **not** resolve it — declared now, so a null is reported as "below this design's
  resolution" and not as an absence.
- Depth: sd 1.67, 30 worlds → SE 0.30, so a 1.0 difference is 3.3 SE. **Resolvable.**

## 5. Refusal rule (fixed before the run)

**Void** if the anchor is not exact, or any of the forty new runs fails structural liveness, or heredity < 0.35 in a cell, or a cell ends with fewer than 28 usable
worlds, or a new world contributes no sister pair.

**The contrast is resolved** if the thirty-seed paired interval excludes zero; **below resolution** if it contains zero with a point estimate smaller than the
design's resolution (0.05 for survival, 1.0 for depth) — reported in those words, not as "no difference"; **contradicted** if it contains zero with a point estimate
larger than the resolution, which would mean the ten-world estimate was inflated and the claim must be withdrawn, not merely qualified.
