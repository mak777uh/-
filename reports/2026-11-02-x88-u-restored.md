## What this does to what we wrote this morning

1. **Claim 295's direction was right and its numbers were wrong.** "Two levels, the band is the shallowest place" is restored: inside
   1.07–1.16, outside 1.82–2.08. But the values in the row (0.56–0.73 inside, 1.47–1.61 outside) came from pooled pairs at five worlds;
   the twenty-world per-world medians are roughly **1.5× larger on both sides**, and the inside edge is 1.07–1.16, not 0.56–0.73.
2. **X87's negative result stands but its extrapolation does not.** X87 measured 0.025–0.035 and said memory declines with inflow; true
   in that range. It also said "the only place a U could still live is the high-inflow arm" — and it does live there. The monotone story
   I wrote at 07:45 is **withdrawn**; the correct statement is a U over 0.025–0.045 with the minimum at 0.035.
3. **Above 0.050 heredity stops existing.** The medium becomes a single percolating maze with no divisions: `ρ₁` = 0.0 on six pairs. That
   is a boundary of the phenomenon, not a measurement problem: a medium that does not divide cannot have lineages.
