## Что убило бы утверждение

- **§5.1, the contact gap (X157).** A re-run whose last-two-component slice is read with a different neck observable (flux, or the minimum along the segment rather than the centre distance minus radii) and which does not give a median near 1.9 cells. Also: a wider logging cadence that makes the 1.32-cell unlogged window larger than the distance between the median and the design's nominal gaps, which would make "the medium's gap" indistinguishable from the design's label.
- **§5.1, the crossing (X158/X164/X165).** Any fused world in the X176/X177 cells (0 of 15 and 0 of 20 are the whole "cliff" argument); a three-body share at gap 10 clearly above the pairwise independence prediction (0.47² = 0.22) — e.g. 3 of 5, which with n = 5 would be indistinguishable from the prediction but is the direction that kills "additive over contacts"; an asymmetric pair whose crossing lies outside the symmetric 10 ± 1 band.
- **§5.1, the one-step band (X166).** A one-step log whose per-arm medians collapse to within ~0.1 of each other, which would restore a point threshold and make the "band" claim the artefact.
- **§5.2, shape heredity (X156).** The rate-invariance reading is already dead (discrepancy 3): it dies at review because the two stored cells disagree by more than their intervals and the report says the inherited aspect swaps. To *save* the sentence one would need a third rate showing the same per-cell slope, which is not in the artifact.
- **§5.2, reversibility (X167).** A control in which a single (never-merged) body divides as fast as the merged one, which would remove "a difference in when" and flatten the test.
- **§5.6, the corridor (X171/X174/X175).** A fourth size whose corridor median lies outside the pooled spread, or a gap arm where the corridor orders opposite to the share; both would break "constant fraction of the peak" and the monotone readout. The existing duplicate arm (X174) means the correlation rests on 7 distinct cells — one more duplicate or a single re-drawn cell can move −0.9492 materially.
- **§5.6, the forecast (X172).** A second unrun supply where the corridor interpolates but the share falls outside 9–15 of 20 again; the item's own verdict is "partial", and one more placement miss ends the single-curve reading.
- **§5.6, the edge (X176–X178).** The whole cliff argument needs 0 fusions one gap past the crossing; a single fused pair in X176 or X177 converts the cliff into a slope and restores the "marginal but alive" cell the band's upper edge was looking for.
- **§5.6, the ladder (X183).** A re-run with the *colder* body's inflow not the best discriminator (AUC ≤ the mean of the two sites), or a ladder whose nominal-rate AUC rises above 0.5 within a level — the latter is impossible by construction and is exactly why the within-level AUC is the informative number.
- **§5.7, the table (X179).** A re-scan that changes the cell count (57) or the run total (772); the article has already recorded 985/722 in the report/plan, so a third number on the same object would make the table's population ambiguous.
- **§5.7, the dispersion (X180).** An outcome that tracks a *different* phase convention once the midpoint is controlled; the mean point-biserial −0.552 is strong, but it is a property of the midpoint reading.
- **§5.8, the withdrawals (X188/X189).** A registered readout whose value moves with its own free parameters *and* the direction reverses — X188 already is that; the fix that would kill the withdrawal is a parameter-free observable. For X189, a second site per level that turns the ±0.004 at radius 3 into a real difference would resurrect the radius-3 half of the prediction.

Needed: the run-level pool behind `x180` (its `runs_scanned = 772`), e.g. a per-run CSV or the `x180` compact slices.
