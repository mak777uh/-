## A. "Fusion stops at a centre separation of 16 ± 1 cells for bodies of radius 3"

- **Вердикт:** держится с оговоркой
- **Что пересчитано:** `l1_field_x136_wall.json → by_separation` plus the 30 raw worlds. Arm size: **yes, 5 seeds per separation** (`runs: 5`, seeds 601–605, `runs: 30` overall; the 30 component traces are all distinct, so these are not duplicate seeds). M/A/P per separation, using the artifact's own fields (`A = absorbed`, `P = both_alive`, `M = the rest`): 12 → M 0 / A 5 / P 0; 14 → 0/5/0; 15 → 0/5/0; 16 → **M 2 / A 2 / P 1**; 17 → **M 2 / A 0 / P 3**; 18 → 0/0/5. Seeds ending as one body (M + A): 5, 5, 5, **4, 2**, 0. Re-reading the raw logs with the x142 definitions instead gives 12 → M 5; 14 → M 5; 15 → M 3 / A 2; 16 → M 2 / A 2 / P 1; 17 → M 2 / P 3; 18 → P 5 — the same merge totals.
- **Расхождение:** the majority stops merging at **separation 17**, one cell above 16: at 16 four of five seeds still end as one body, at 17 only two. Published 16 ± 1; recomputed 17 ± 1 (equivalently: the switch lies between 16 and 17, width 1 cell). The printed sentence "At 16 cells they fuse in two of five; at 17, in none" quotes the **A-only** counts: x142's own arm at 16 is `r30_g10 = M 2/5, A 2/5, P 1/5`, i.e. 4/5 fuse, and at 17 (`r30_g11`) 2/5 fuse.
- **Проверки на устойчивость:** the timing readout cross-checks: `by_separation.median_time` = 19 / 39 / 49 / 79 steps at separations 12 / 14 / 15 / 16, exactly the four times printed in §4.1. `x136` labels 12, 14 and 15 as "absorbed 5/5" because its absorption test accepts the partner logged at the *same* step (`o[0] >= t_end`, `scripts/x136_analyse.py:74`), so an equal-step mutual fusion counts as absorption. In the raw worlds uids 1 and 2 are last logged at the *same* step (20 / 40 / 60 at separations 12 / 14 / 15), i.e. they end together. Traced `x142_g9_r30_s601`: two 136-cell bodies (uids 1, 2) at step 60, one 285-cell body (uid 3) at step 70 — both lineages end together and a new uid appears, which is M, not A. The switch itself is unaffected: `M + A` is 5, 5, 5, 4, 2, 0 under both readings.
- **Что убьёт утверждение:** a re-run of separation 16 with more seeds giving ≤ 2/5 one-body endings (switch moves to 16 or below), or of 17 giving ≥ 3/5 (switch moves to 18). Independently: any referee who reads "fusion" as M + A (the paper's own definition in §4.2) will recompute 17, and the printed counts 2/5 and 0/5 are already contradicted by `x142` arm `r30_g10`.

## B. "The surface gap at which fusion stops is 9 ± 1 cells, the same for bodies of 29, 64 and 113 cells"

- **Вердикт:** не держится
- **Что пересчитано:** `by_arm` of `x142` (r 3: gaps 1–11; r 4.5: gaps 4–7), `x147` (r 4.5: gaps 7, 8, 9, 11, 13) and `x150` (r 6: gaps 6, 7, 8); all arms 5 seeds. Arithmetic: `g = d − 2r` with the nominal radii. Radius 3: `16 − 2·3 = 10`; radius 4.5: `18 − 2·4.5 = 9`; radius 6: `20 − 2·6 = 8`. Checks asked for: gap 8 is **M 5/5** and gap 9 is **M 3/5, A 2/5** at radius 3 — both confirmed (`r30_g8`, `r30_g9`).
- **Расхождение:** the three printed gaps 10 / 9 / 8 are arithmetic from the chosen separations, not measured stopping points. Radius 3: gap 8 M 5/5; 9 M 3 / A 2 (still 5/5 one body); 10 M 2 / A 2 / P 1 (**4/5**); 11 M 2 / P 3 (**2/5**). Radius 4.5: 7 M 5/5; 8 M 3 / A 2 (5/5); 9 M 2 / A 2 / P 1 (**4/5**); 11 P 5 (0/5); gap 10 not run. Radius 6: 6 M 5/5; 7 M 5/5; 8 M 3 / A 2 → **5/5 still end as one body at separation 20**, and there is no arm at gap 9 or 10. So at every one of the three quoted gaps a majority still fused, and for radius 6 all five did. Arithmetically 10 and 8 are inside 9 ± 1, so the band is not in dispute; what is unsupported is calling any of the three a gap at which fusion stops, and no single criterion produces 10 / 9 / 8. The mutual-fusion (M 5/5) crossover is 9 / 8 / 8 — a one-cell step, not "the same gap"; the last-majority gap is 10 / 9 / ≥ 8; the first gap at which a majority no longer fuses is 11 / 11 / unmeasured.
- **Проверки на устойчивость:** the artifacts' own boundary field says the same thing: `x150.boundary.largest_gap_all_coalesce` = **r30 8, r45 7, r60 7** (largest gap with every seed M), not 10 / 9 / 8. A second, independent problem of definition: Box 1 defines the gap from the bodies' **effective** radii, but `x142.definitions.gap` computes `SEP − 2R` from the **nominal** radii. The discs grow from 26 / 62 / 113 cells at seeding to 120–136 cells each before fusing (traced g9 run: 136 cells each at d = 15, i.e. r_eff ≈ 6.6 and a true surface gap ≈ 1.8 cells), so "9 cells" is a nominal-radius quantity, not a distance between surfaces. Seeded areas are 26 / 62 / 113, so the "29 cells" label is the typical dying-body area, not the seeded disc.
- **Что убьёт утверждение:** an r = 6 arm at gap 9 or 10 with ≥ 3/5 one-body endings (the existing 5/5 at gap 8 makes this the likely outcome and would already refute "stops at 8"); an r = 3 arm at gap 11 with ≥ 3/5 (moves the r 3 stop to 11). Recomputing the gap with effective radii as defined in Box 1 moves the conserved number from ~9 to ~0–2 cells and removes the 12-cell-wavelength comparison.

## C. "The daughter-on-parent slope of the tracked trait is 0.9837 [0.9680, 1.0002] in the F = 0.030 habitat"

- **Вердикт:** держится
- **Что пересчитано:** the ten raw F = 0.030 worlds, `x108_f030_s301..s310.json`, using `uid_pairs` for parentage and `series[*].components_log` for `s`, with the parent read at its last logged step ≤ the daughter's first (median lag 10 steps, no same-step pair). **2 164 pairs found** out of 2 211 edges (per world 159 / 170 / 389 / 152 / 282 / 179 / 190 / 196 / 159 / 288), over **1 073 parent uids**. OLS slope of daughter on parent = **0.9837151364976248**; residual sd of daughter − parent = **0.14868**.
- **Расхождение:** none in the point estimate — 0.98372 against the published 0.9837, difference < 0.0001, far inside the 0.01 tolerance. Interval: my 2 000-resample cluster bootstrap over parent uids gives **[0.9687, 0.9996]** (seed 12345); across five bootstrap seeds the 2.5 % end is 0.9679–0.9687 and the 97.5 % end 0.9985–1.0002, so the published [0.9680, 1.0002] is one draw of the same procedure and is compatible. Note that the upper end sits on 1.0 and can fall just below with another seed: "slope = 1" is at the boundary of the interval, not comfortably inside it.
- **Проверки на устойчивость:** the parent map is not taken on trust — for the 254 unambiguous split events (exactly two new uids and one vanished body at a logged step, both new uids within 15 cells of it) a purely positional reconstruction returns 508 pairs whose parent is, in **508/508** cases, the `uid_pairs` parent (subset slope 0.9941, biased by the selection, so not comparable to 0.9837). I did not check the connected-habitat slope 0.9931, which is outside claim C.
- **Что убьёт утверждение:** a parent–daughter reconstruction that does not use `uid_pairs` and yields |Δslope| > 0.01; or evidence that some `uid_pairs` edges are fusion rather than split edges (a daughter with two parents, or a daughter read at the same step as the parent, which would force the slope to 1 by construction). Neither was found: no same-step pairs, and every clean split event has one vanished parent.
