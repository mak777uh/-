# Проверка: at gap 11 the fusion shares are 0/10, 1/10 and 3/10, and the upward trend across inflow rates is noise

- **Раздел/строка CLAIMS:** `reports/2026-11-04-x168d-gap11.md` — the Result table and the sentence "**Verdict: the trend was noise**"; aggregate `morphogenesis/experiments/E09_pond/l1_field_x168d_gap11.json`; pre-registration `reports/2026-11-04-x168d-gap11-plan.md` §4–5.
- **Вердикт:** не держится
- **Что пересчитано:** all 60 raw runs `morphogenesis/experiments/L1_field/x168d_F{26,30,36}_g{8,11}_s961..970.json`: 201 sampled rows each (steps 1–2000; `components` is present on every row and equals `len(components_log)`), `min(components)` per run, shares, Wilson 95 % intervals (z = 1.96), and two-sided Fisher exact tests by summing hypergeometric probabilities with `math.comb`, cross-checked with `scipy.stats.fisher_exact`. I compared the aggregate `by_cell` cell by cell, recomputed the exact permutation trend test over the three ordered rates, and recomputed exact Fisher power at n = 10.
- **Расхождение:** the report's table and tests reproduce exactly — 0/10, 1/10, 3/10; Wilson [0, 0.2775], [0.0179, 0.4042], [0.1078, 0.6032]; Fisher 0.026 vs 0.030 = 1.00, 0.030 vs 0.036 = 0.58204, 0.026 vs 0.036 = 0.21053; controls 10/10; no run with `min == 0`. Two other things do not: (1) aggregate `extreme_contrast.difference` = **0.01**, not 0.30 — `scripts/x168d_analyse.py:89` subtracts the flow values (0.036 − 0.026) instead of the shares (`g11[hi]["share"] - g11[lo]["share"]` = 0.30), and the branch `extreme["difference"] >= 0.3` at line 99 is therefore unreachable for any data, so the "trend is real" verdict can never be emitted; (2) the plan's §4 claim of "about 80 % power" for a 0.3 difference at n = 10 is wrong: exact two-sided Fisher power is **0.150** for 0.0 vs 0.3, 0.132 for 0.4 vs 0.7 and 0.132 for 0.5 vs 0.8 (0.0 vs 0.4: 0.367). No 0.3 contrast at n = 10 reaches 80 %.
- **Проверки на устойчивость:** (a) `min == 1` is a genuine coalescence marker in these runs: for all 34 runs with `min == 1` the first one-component sample has one area 0.95–1.20× the sum of the two previous areas and a centroid within 0.6 cells of their midpoint; in most controls the two initial uids end together and a new merged body appears, and in the rest one uid absorbs the other (its area roughly doubles at the merge step). No `min == 1` is a one-body death. (b) But the one-component state is transient: every one of the 60 runs ends with **22–56 components**. Under "one body at the final step" the gap-8 control is **0/30**, not 10/10, and the experiment would fail its own control gate. `min == 1` only means "the two seeded bodies coalesced at least once". (c) Fisher on the extreme pair is exact for that 2×2 table but uses only two of the three arms and is less powerful than an ordered trend test for a monotone hypothesis. Exact permutation/Cochran–Armitage over all three ordered rates (scores 0.026/0.030/0.036 or ranks 1/2/3 give the same answer): one-sided p = **0.0515**, two-sided 0.103 (asymptotic CA z = 2.00, two-sided p = 0.045, liberal). A ladder 0/10, 1/10, 4/10 gives one-sided p = 0.0165. (d) Three pairwise tests: Holm/Bonferroni on the smallest p = 0.21 gives 0.63, so correction does not change "not significant", but p = 0.21 is an uncorrected extreme-pair value. (e) "No contrast clears a 0.3 difference" is vacuous: only the extreme pair has a 0.3 difference; the others are 0.1 and 0.2. (f) The pre-registered refusal rule §5 has two branches (flat and within the X158 interval → noise; extreme ≥ 0.3 and p < 0.05 → real); 0.3 with p = 0.21 falls in neither, and the code defaults to "noise". The X158 comparison promised in the first branch is not implemented (the baseline is stored, no test is run). Plan §3's last-two-body-gap spread is also missing from the aggregate; I recomputed it: sd = 0.99–2.56 cells across the six cells, so it would pass, but it is unreported.
- **Что убьёт утверждение:** one more fused run at F = 0.036 (a 4/10 ladder) moves the exact ordered one-sided p from 0.051 to 0.017 and the verdict to "trend"; the report's own 40-seed arm is the direct test. Independently, recomputing "fused" as "one body at the final step" makes the control 0/30, so the 10/10 control claim depends entirely on the transient definition. And fixing `difference` to `share_hi − share_lo` and re-running the analysis would show that the published verdict came from the fallback branch, not from the pre-registered rule.
- **Что предложить ведущему:** (1) replace the headline with "no trend detected: 0/10, 1/10, 3/10; exact ordered test one-sided p ≈ 0.05; the design had ~15 % power for the pre-registered 0.3 effect", and drop "the flat result now means what 'no trend' is supposed to mean" from the report and from the `X168d_gap11_ten_seeds` queue note; (2) fix `scripts/x168d_analyse.py:89` to use the share difference and re-run only the analysis (the numbers will not change, the verdict branch will); (3) add the 60 per-run artifacts to git — they are currently untracked and only `x168d_series_index.json` is tracked; (4) add this file to `lab/reviews/index.json`, which I was told not to touch.

All six `by_cell` cells match exactly: same `mins` arrays, `runs`, `fused`, `died`, `share`, `wilson`; `fisher_tests` matches to five decimals; `controls.per_rate` is true for all three rates. The `min == 1` definition is applied consistently by `x168c_analyse.py` and `x168d_analyse.py`, and I found no false positive from one-body death. The statement "no run went extinct" is true (`min == 0` never occurs). If the claim is read narrowly as "the observed numbers are 0/10, 1/10, 3/10 and Fisher gives p = 0.21", it holds.

## What does not hold

The headline sentence is an **absence** claim, and the design cannot support it. The report says "the flat result now means what 'no trend' is supposed to mean, rather than 'we could not see one'". At n = 10 per arm, a 0.3 difference has 15 % power, so "we could not see one" is exactly what the design delivers: a 0.3 effect is missed 85 % of the time. The observed 0/10 vs 3/10 is the typical outcome of a 15 %-power experiment whether or not a trend exists. The Limits section concedes that 0.2 would be missed but does not concede that 0.3 — the pre-registered effect size — is also missed. The correct conclusion is "no trend detected at the pre-registered test", not "the trend was noise", and the queue note's stronger "measured rather than power-limited" is unsupported.

The second failure is procedural: the aggregate's `extreme_contrast.difference` is 0.01, the flow spacing, not 0.30, the share spacing. The decision branch that requires `difference >= 0.3` is dead code, so the verdict is the fallback `else`, not the pre-registered criterion. It happens that the fixed criterion would also fail (p = 0.21), but the report should not be able to claim that from a field that never reached 0.3. A third, smaller wording error: the report calls the 30-percentage-point contrast "below the pre-registered bar of a 0.3 difference at p < 0.05". It is *at* the 0.3 difference bar, not below it; the bar is missed on p (0.21 > 0.05), and the sentence should say so, otherwise it contradicts the table two lines above.

## Reproducibility chain

- **Aggregate vs raw:** exact agreement in `by_cell`, shares, Wilson, Fisher, controls; only `extreme_contrast.difference` is wrong (0.01 vs 0.30).
- **Ambiguous runs:** none. Every one of the 60 runs has `components` on all 201 rows, reaches step 2000, and never ends with two bodies; no `.compact.json` duplicates are present in the glob.
- **Operationalisation:** `min == 1` is sound for "the two bodies coalesced at least once" (all 34 cases are area/centroid-consistent mergers), but not for "fused into a persistent one-body state" (all 60 end with 22–56 components; the controls included).
- **Git:** the 60 per-run files are untracked and `git check-ignore` does not ignore them; only `x168d_series_index.json` is tracked. A fresh clone gets the report and the aggregate but cannot recompute the table.

## The strongest objection from an outside referee

"The report mistakes non-significance for evidence of absence, and its own pre-registration set an 80 % power target that the design misses by a factor of five. With ten seeds per arm a 0.3 difference is detected 15 % of the time; p = 0.21 is what that design produces whether or not a trend exists. A proper ordered test on the same 30 runs gives one-sided p = 0.05, so the honest statement is 'no trend detected', not 'the trend was noise'. The word 'fused' is also doing more work than the data: every run, including the 10/10 controls, ends with tens of components, so what was measured is a transient coalescence, not a fused body."
