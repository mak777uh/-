## What the document argues, in its own terms

1. **No field-wide numerical threshold for "life-like" exists.** The defensible minimum is a Darwinian test at a
   stated level of organisation: heritable variation in a fitness-related trait **and** a measurable response to
   selection distinguishable from a matched drift/neutral control.
2. **Interaction, reproduction-with-heredity and open-endedness are three different claims.** A reaction–diffusion
   structure can persist, interact, merge and split without demonstrating heredity.
3. **`h²` is the wrong default for clonal digital systems;** broad-sense `H² = V_G/V_P` is the appropriate quantity,
   because it keeps non-additive transmissible variance that narrow-sense `h²` discards.
4. **Published thresholds, where they exist, are selection-coefficient thresholds, not `h²` thresholds** — its
   examples (second-hand): Avida, fitness effects ≤ 0.075 unresponsive and ≈ 0.2 drift-dominated; one
   resource-bounded digital ecology with `s = 0.00615` (95 % CI 0.00596–0.00634), `sN_e` 0.098–0.173, directional
   selection in 7 of 8 conditions and no shift when mutation was ablated.
5. **Four controls are mandatory**, and the fourth is the one we have never run: shuffled-parent null; inheritance
   ablated; environment replayed; randomised event timing.
6. **Coalescence is a merger, not a birth.** Every structure-level event should carry a type — division,
   fragmentation, coalescence, fusion-then-division, death, persistence — and the hardest case (fusion then
   division) should be kept as an event graph rather than forced into a single-parent tree.
7. **When a merger destroys lineage identity** is an evolutionary criterion, not a physical threshold: the originals
   stop being independently identifiable reproducing units. Graded individuality (Godfrey-Smith) is the vocabulary.

## What is genuinely new for us

1. **`H²` as a variance ratio, with the shuffle null, is a different statistic from our `r`.** We have never
   computed it, and the difference is not cosmetic: `r ≈ 0.95` says "daughters resemble parents", which the copying
   rule guarantees; `H² = V_G/V_P` with a shuffled-parent null asks how much of the *between-lineage* variance is
   transmissible, and is falsifiable. This is the first quantitative heredity number our field line could own.
2. **A selection response `R` with a sham-selection control** is a second, independent readout. We have `S1`
   (drift against a null), which is the same logic on a different quantity; the document's `R` is cheaper to compute
   from the same logs and can be reported alongside.
3. **The event vocabulary is prior art we can cite for our own M/A/P typing.** The document's specific negative
   claim — the Gray–Scott literature does not do lineage accounting, so coalescence and reproduction are conflated
   there — is a sentence our §10hn discussion can use, *provided* S2/S3 are read first (both are `[А]` today).

## What we should not adopt

- **MODES and Bedau activity as pass/fail.** They are longitudinal system-level descriptions with no accepted
  threshold; adding them would create a new claim type that our manuscript does not need.
- **Any `h²` cutoff** (0.1, 0.3, 0.5). The document is explicit that these are not conventions, and it failed to
  find a digital system that reports `h²` with an accepted cutoff. If we ever quote a threshold, it must be
  system-specific, registered before the run, and paired with a null.
- **Its second-hand numbers as citations.** The Avida 0.075/0.2 thresholds, the `s = 0.00615` ecology and the
  robot slopes 0.35–0.83 are all `[А]`; none may enter a claim, README row or manuscript fragment until the primary
  text is in `library/corpus` and read.

## Proposed next experiment (needs the owner's frame decision before any run)

**X155 — is the field line's heredity mechanical or transmissible?** Analysis of existing artifacts first, one
cheap ablation arm second.

- **Data already on disk.** Full artifacts store per-component `s` at every logged step and `uid_pairs`; a
  parent–daughter pair can therefore be reconstructed exactly (the `selection` block keeps only `r` and the pair
  count, so the pairs themselves must be recomputed). **Caveat:** the compact files in git keep `[uid, area, cx, cy]`
  and drop `s` — a `compact` v2 that keeps `s` is a prerequisite for the analysis to be reproducible from the
  repository alone.
- **Phase 1 (0 GPU).** Per run: build `(s_parent, s_daughter)` pairs; report the parent–offspring regression slope
  with its bootstrap CI over uids and over runs; `H² = V_G/V_P` with `V_G` from a lineage decomposition; the
  **shuffled-parent null** (permute parent assignment within run); and the slope **partialled on division rate**,
  because `r` is already known to track it.
- **Phase 2 (≈0.1 GPU-h).** Two runs with `--mutation 0` at the same `F`, radius and horizon: if the response
  survives with mutation ablated, the pattern is environmental reconstruction, not heredity (§5.5 of the document,
  control 2).
- **Refusal rule, registered before the run.** Claim "transmissible heredity" **only** if (a) the slope's CI
  excludes 0, (b) the shuffle null is rejected at 95 %, (c) the division-rate-partialled slope is still positive and
  its CI excludes 0, and (d) the mutation-off arm shows no drift beyond the null. Any failure is reported as the
  corresponding negative: mechanical, environmental, or below resolution.
- **What it would let us write.** One sentence in §10hn's block and one claim row with an evidence type, replacing
  the present honest "we measured transmission, not heritability".
