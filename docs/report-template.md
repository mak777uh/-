# Report template — an observation, not an argument

Every report written from 08.11 onwards follows this shape. The shape is enforced by
`scripts/check_report_style.py`.

The reason for the shape is in the reading: an argument reaches only the conscious mind, and the
conscious mind resists (P294); a report full of adjectives and comparatives is sentimentality, and
sentimentality avoids facts (P130, P144); a report whose sections are "the mechanism worked / did not work"
is a fragment and cannot see the whole (P220). What a report can do is **describe what was seen**, and say
honestly what it does not know.

---

## 1. What we looked at

One or two sentences. **No mechanism in the sentence.** If the sentence needs a mechanism to make sense, the
report is about the mechanism, not about the world.

Good: "We watched what a cell does when the food moves away from it."
Bad: "We tested whether the memory field improves the favourable share."

## 2. What we saw

The curves and the numbers. **The first 20 steps are mandatory** (P4, P186). The three readouts are
mandatory: `situation_explained`, `record_explained`, `energy_per_behaviour`.

Report **the cell's own measures** (energy, survival, offspring) next to ours (P27, P59). If they disagree,
the disagreement is the finding.

**No adjectives. No comparatives.** Not "a stronger effect", but "0.012 in arm A and 0.003 in arm B".

## 3. What it means

A description of what the world does — **not** an evaluation of whether a mechanism worked.

If the result contradicts the design, **it is a finding about the design** (P123), not a failure of the
mechanism.

## 4. What we do not know

A list. **If the list is short, this was a theology** (P127).

## 5. What this changes in the next experiment

A concrete change. **If the answer is "nothing", the experiment was a pointing out** (P294) and should be
said so.

---

## Forbidden in a report

| forbidden | why |
|---|---|
| adjectives of magnitude ("strong", "weak", "huge", "tiny") | P130: sentimentality avoids facts |
| comparatives ("stronger", "deeper", "more significant") | P144: comparison is a waste of energy |
| "breakthrough", "first", "important", "novel" | P164: a finding needs no author |
| moral verdicts ("the design was wrong") | P161: "it is wrong" is a concept, not a fact |
| "we should look into this" as a conclusion | P180: the proof of seeing is the action |
| a school, a mechanism, a previous position defended | P246: belonging is not intelligence |

## Required in a report

| required | why |
|---|---|
| the first 20 steps | P4, P186: the silence is at the beginning |
| the three readouts | P80, P128, P255: the within-cell measures |
| the cell's own measures | P27, P59: our metric is a fragment with a crown |
| a list of what we do not know | P127 |
| a concrete change for the next experiment | P235: relate the fact to the action |
